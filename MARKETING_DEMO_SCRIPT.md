# 30-second demo script — CreatorFlow365

**Use for:** screen recording, live demo, social posts  
**URL:** https://www.creatorflow365.com  
**CTA:** Create free account → Documents

---

## Script (read while clicking)

1. **Home** — “One draft, many exports. I save my original once.”
2. **Create free account** — sign up (no card).
3. **Documents** — paste your caption or notes.
4. **Save** — give it a name.
5. **Pick Instagram** (or any platform).
6. **Copy formatted** — paste into the app you post from.

**Close:** “Free while we build at creatorflow365.com.”

---

## On-screen path

`/signup` → `/documents` → save → platform panel → **Copy formatted**
